  - With a 2-pt margin range, the midpoint sits below 18.15% whenever the top is **below 19.15%**. A range of 17-19% "matches consensus at the top" but has an 18.0% midpoint. A 16-18% range (top ≈ consensus) has a 17.0% midpoint, 1.15 pts below.

## 4. Arithmetic

4. **Share of the two-year step taken in year one:** (18.15 − 13)/(20 − 13) = **73.6%**; (18.15 − 13.74)/(20 − 13.74) = **70.4%**.

**Reasoning behind each bucket (research judgement):**
- **<15% (5%):** needs a Wind or Q4 execution problem, or a deliberately low guide. GEV has never cut company revenue or FCF guidance, and a guide below FY26's exit would contradict "'27 much better" for Wind.
- **15-16% (10%):** the straight line from FY26 actuals minus about 1 pt of cushion (Case A). This fits the "grounded" style and the CEO's timing of margin step-ups to '29-'30.
- **16-17% (25%):** the straight line (16.5-16.9%), or a 16-18% range. This is what a 2-pt range with its top near consensus looks like, and it leaves room under a 20% 2028 plan.
- **17-18% (30%):** centre of the "first guide vs prior Q4" history (17.8%) and Case B. It lets management guide a big step while keeping the 2028 framework credible before the CMD.
- **18-19% (22%):** mostly a 17-19% range (18.0% midpoint). It would be justified by the ~18% Q4'26 exit and H1'26 Power at 17.6-18.8%. It implies only about +2 pts to 2028, which effectively signals a framework raise.
- **≥19% (8%):** requires guiding above consensus and pulling 2028 forward. GEV did something similar with the margin at Q2'25 and Q1'26, but never on a first annual guide.
