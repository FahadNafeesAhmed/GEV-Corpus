## 5. General question bank items answered

- **G2.** On a delivered basis, the largest company H2−H1 step so far is +2.2 pts. Consensus FY26 needs +5.8 pts, but about 3.2 pts of that is the Wind swing from H1 (−19% margin) to H2 (positive).
- **G13.** The Q3 print (10-28) is the first dated read on the FY26 base. The pass marks are in Q7.
- **G16.** Wind is being treated as noise. FY26 consensus implicitly assumes Wind delivers a quarter more than 13× its best ever. Q4'25 is the precedent: Street Q4 EBITDA was too high by 10.9% on Wind and offshore losses.
- **G20.** The quarterly consensus history rests mostly on StockStory data. Q1'26 is single-source. The FY23 quarters rest on one release each.

## 6. What would change this

- **Q3 Wind below about −$100M, or the FY Wind guide widened.** The base then likely lands at or below the guide midpoint, and the base share of the gap rises toward 37%+.
- **Q3 Power ≥ 18.5% or Electrification ≥ 20%.** This supports the segment-top FY26 (~$6.5–6.7B). The research growth path would then sit only −3.5% to −5% below consensus FY27.
- **A disclosed Vineyard settlement gain or loss** could move Wind H2 by an amount that has not been quantified.
- **Prolec seasonality** differing from legacy Electrification would weaken the Electrification seasonal inference.

## 7. Disagreements with the master brief

1. The brief's framing suggests FY26 consensus is stretched. **The evidence says the stretch is almost entirely Wind.** Power and Electrification seasonality support consensus; the issue is not company-wide margin.
2. "Never beaten first margin guide": against the *final* guide, FY24 finished 5bp above the midpoint and FY25 10bp below it. The company margin lands near the midpoint; it is neither a beat nor a miss pattern.
3. Quarterly EBITDA versus consensus is not a one-way record. There were two misses (Q4'25 −10.9%, Q2'26 ~−2%) and two beats (Q3'25, Q1'26).
